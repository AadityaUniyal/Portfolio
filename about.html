THE THREAD

Build the whole product. Stay curious about every layer.

That shows up in different ways across my projects: checking whether an interface works with a keyboard, grounding an AI result in its source, connecting a forecast to an operational decision, or making cloud concepts tangible on a local machine.

I like moving between product questions and engineering details. What does the person need to do? How should the system respond? What happens in the layers underneath? And how can we make the result clear enough to trust?

WHAT I LIKE TO WORK THROUGH
01 / PRODUCT

Follow the real workflow

Start with the task and its friction. Build the product around what needs to happen, not just the technology available.

02 / SYSTEMS

Connect the layers

Move between UI, API, data, models, and infrastructure to understand how the whole product behaves.

03 / RELIABILITY

Make the result legible

Use checks, provenance, and clear feedback so people can inspect what the system decided and why.
